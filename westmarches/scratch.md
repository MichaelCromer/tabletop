I met her in the flesh only once, to seal the compact. I never wish to do so again.  She
smiled with her teeth but never blinked. When she reached out to shake my hand, where
she touched felt colder for weeks.  She came from beyond the veil of dream — but not
just any dream. One must be willing.  One must be still.

To meet her, I lay within the old sarcophagus beneath the blossom-covered mound.  I
drank three drops of the silver tincture, nothing more. Then I closed my eyes and
invited her in.

The wind died. My breath froze in my throat. When I opened my eyes, I was standing in
the ruin where her hive dwells.  The moon was black. It was raining ash.  

Morwenna wants the mead to carry her whispers — not just to the dreamers, but to the
waking world.  She promised endless honey if I dose a few batches.

I tried to stop.  Burned the barrels. Blocked the tombs. But the blossoms keep blooming.

She won’t let me rest.

I only hope someone stronger finds this. Drink only if you wish to see her.  And only if
