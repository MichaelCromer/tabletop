Old Town 

Surrounded by pale stone walls, at the crest of a hill. Densely built stone houses with
slate roofs, ivy-covered walls.  Steep, narrow streets.  Many officials and or noble
families.

New Town

Downslope from Old Town, surrounded on remaining three sides by walls of amber
sandstone. Spacious, broad roads and houses of wood and tile. Originally craftspeople
and traders, now the main economic centre.
